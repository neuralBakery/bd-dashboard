<h1>Investing in fast-growing businesses to keep succeeding</h1>
02/11/2026
<br /><br />
<div><p paraid="1759028683" paraeid="{e757dce2-97b2-41db-83a8-77cbf4a6397a}{177}">Right across our regions, diverse businesses are looking to grow their operations &ndash; creating jobs, boosting local supply chains and generating new economic activity.</p></div><div><p paraid="2046733832" paraeid="{e757dce2-97b2-41db-83a8-77cbf4a6397a}{194}">We&rsquo;re supporting this aspiration through the $150 million <a href="https://www.rdv.vic.gov.au/grants/victorian-investment-fund" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Victorian Investment Fund</a>.</p></div><div><p paraid="51654052" paraeid="{e757dce2-97b2-41db-83a8-77cbf4a6397a}{212}">This includes in Bendigo, where local manufacturer <a href="https://bentrol.com.au" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Bentrol</strong></a> builds switchboards and switchrooms &ndash; essential for major infrastructure like utilities, water, resources and mining projects.</p></div><div><p paraid="29547032" paraeid="{e757dce2-97b2-41db-83a8-77cbf4a6397a}{251}">The business has been on a growth run since it started in 2011, and it's about to build a new headquarters in East Bendigo. This expansion will see Bentrol increase production capacity and bring on 85 new jobs, including 17 apprenticeships.</p></div><div><p paraid="1664853541" paraeid="{64233a8c-0bf9-48fa-b2fe-51e919cdbf7f}{53}">At nearby Lockwood, there are more jobs on the way. Poultry producer <a href="https://hazeldenes.com.au" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Hazeldenes</strong></a> will create more than 140 positions through its investment in water infrastructure and sustainability. Backed by our fund, the project will drive a 76% increase in production for Hazeldenes, which is a key part of the regional food-production industry.</p></div><div><div><p paraid="2061690429" paraeid="{64233a8c-0bf9-48fa-b2fe-51e919cdbf7f}{125}">Over in Ballarat we're backing more food and more success at <a href="https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESnAEB6zswFYEdX1TpP2kKslQeoISDmsN5RQhyy1GlfIOEhWBv-NCSj2mE_nswVfBl0uq13pX0q1dU_fVEzYXsd5WB6NVEzi5TjJuyoQLCDq6sfWaTQc6UTZM8wiw6J6Xr_RZ4JBT1aXRYqd2J1w0mj8-U7KOXkW0B1A0U-DQ3HpdnyImjCCddw4JzdyIFgy6XFnp__atCWOQgp_Xk9Qw" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Exotic Bazaar</strong></a>. Originally a home-based business, it's now ready to expand into a new commercial headquarters and production site. Here it will create 18 jobs (and more of its delicious recipe bases, spice blends, baking kits and gift ranges).</p></div><div><p paraid="1740282634" paraeid="{64233a8c-0bf9-48fa-b2fe-51e919cdbf7f}{177}">Niche technology is being developed in Geelong, where <a href="https://healthrfid.com" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Health RFID</strong></a> aims to reach more international customers and create skilled jobs through a body-tracking simulator. The simulator will improve efficiency at specialised health and research settings such as mortuaries.</p></div><div><p paraid="446706623" paraeid="{64233a8c-0bf9-48fa-b2fe-51e919cdbf7f}{241}">These
