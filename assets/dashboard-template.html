Weekly research routine

Use this checklist once per week

Not an automatic live feed. These searches open current public websites. Add or revise findings after checking original sources and dates. Existing seeded results remain a dated snapshot.
    A weekly review mark records that you completed the process; it does not assert that every source has been checked or that no new developments occurred.

    Research triage

    Finding: Confirm factual statement and date against a primary source where possible.

    Commercial relevance: Distinguish a hiring need from a labour hire buying signal.

    Decision-maker: Name the person only where the identity and role are publicly supported and current.

    Next step: Add a personal note or monitor for construction award, commissioning or actual vacancies.

    Source directory

    Starting points for weekly desk research, including government procurement and regional media.

    Evidence standard: Company websites and official government/tender sources are preferred. Media and third-party job boards are useful early signals but should be rechecked. The dashboard does not scrape restricted sites or guarantee complete coverage.
